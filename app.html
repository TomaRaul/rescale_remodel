Create and modify · shapes and text

Create, move, split, write, text box. The canvas size is requested in the box on the left. Undo reverses the last change, whichever of the two it was.
Shapes, texts and boxes can also be moved directly on the canvas: hold the left button on them and drag. Dragged from empty space, the same gesture draws a selection rectangle — everything it touches joins the selection and then moves together. Shift adds to what was already selected. One drag is a single change, so Undo reverses all of it.
